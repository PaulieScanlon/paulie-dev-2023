Mastra Factory now supports three new intakes: [Jira](https://factory.mastra.ai/configure/jira), [GitLab](https://factory.mastra.ai/configure/gitlab), and [incident.io](https://factory.mastra.ai/configure/incidentio), giving you more ways to manage and resolve issues, feature requests, and incident follow-ups.

We launched [Mastra Factory](https://mastra.ai/blog/announcing-mastra-factory) earlier this year with support for GitHub, Linear, and Slack, and followed up with the [beta](https://mastra.ai/blog/announcing-mastra-factory-beta) launch earlier this month. Today, Jira, GitLab and incident.io join the intake lineup.

The intake column in Factory can be filtered by source, letting you see where each issue has come from. Open an item to read the full issue description, then move it through your pipeline until the work is complete.

Watch our recent workshop [Build and Own Your Software Factory](https://www.youtube.com/live/LzZ_FptHWqs?si=jPRw7FdghAKXOavn) to learn more.
